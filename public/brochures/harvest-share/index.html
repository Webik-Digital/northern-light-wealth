Harvest Share

The Idea

Wealth is rarely
created in isolation.

Wealth creates opportunity. It also creates the ability to participate beyond ourselves.

Businesses and families are shaped by employees, customers, teachers, coaches, neighbours, organizations and communities.

There is no prescribed obligation. Wealth simply widens the circle of people and places a family has reason to notice.

Harvest Share allows Northern Light Wealth to share part of the economic success of the relationship while participating clients choose where their Harvest Share amount is directed.

Sometimes that may mean supporting a registered charity. Sometimes it may mean helping fund something tangible in the community.

Success creates more choices
about where time, attention
and resources can go.

Gratitude does not need to be performative to become action.

The Firm Participates

Responsibility should
begin with us

Harvest Share is how we put part of our own economics behind that belief.

Northern Light Wealth is a business. We believe in being fairly compensated for the advice, investment stewardship, coordination and responsibility entrusted to us.

We also believe success can create an opportunity to participate beyond ourselves.

  1. 1

    Fees continue normally

    Management fees continue to be charged under your applicable Northern Light Wealth fee arrangement. Nothing about that changes.

  2. 2

    Calculated annually

    After the annual fee period is complete and management fees have been fully calculated and charged, we calculate the participating client’s Harvest Share amount under the program terms.

  3. 3

    Northern Light participates

    Northern Light Wealth funds the Harvest Share amount from its own account. The participating client identifies where they would like it directed.

A normal fee relationship. An annual Harvest Share calculation. Northern Light Wealth funds the amount. You choose where you would like it directed.

Participation is always optional.

Clients who do not participate simply continue under their normal Northern Light Wealth fee arrangement.

Harvest Share

Your Share. Your Choice.

What matters to your family
may not look like philanthropy

Harvest Share is intentionally broad.

A family may choose to support a registered Canadian charity. Another may want to help a local community organization. Another may see something very tangible that would make their community better.

Northern Light Wealth does not maintain a preferred list and does not choose the recipient.

Charitable receipting

Harvest Share does not require the recipient to be a registered charity. Where a participating client chooses an eligible registered charity, charitable receipting may be available through that organization, and where applicable the receipt is associated with the participating client. That may be useful, but it is not the purpose of Harvest Share and it is not a requirement for participation.

Something tangible

  • New boards at the neighbourhood rink
  • Equipment for a local program
  • Support for a community school or team
  • A community project that otherwise may not happen
  • An organization that helped their family
  • A cause connected to something they have experienced

Examples only

Our participation. Your choice.

Harvest Share

Participation Can Teach

Especially when the family
is invited into it

Harvest Share can create conversations that might otherwise never happen.

These are stewardship questions

  • What matters to us?
  • Who helped us get here?
  • What have we benefited from that we would like someone else to experience?
  • What would the kids choose?
  • What does our community need?
  • What does responsibility look like when there is no requirement to act?

Children and grandchildren may bring forward different ideas, and different generations may care about entirely different things. The outcome is not necessarily agreement. The value is participation — helping future stewards understand that wealth creates choices, and that some of those choices can extend beyond ourselves.

Over time

  1. Conversation becomes participation
  2. Participation becomes experience
  3. Experience becomes judgment
  4. Judgment can become wisdom

Responsibility is easier to understand when you have had the opportunity to practise it.

Sometimes the lasting value may be the conversation that happened around the kitchen table before the decision was made.
