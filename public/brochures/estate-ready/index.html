EstateReady
Before an estate exists

Your Will may be ready.
Is your Executor?

One day, someone you trust will have to step in while they are grieving. They should not have to start from scratch.

Before that day, there is time to talk.

Make sure your Executor knows they have been chosen, where the Will is, who to call and what matters to you.

Give your Executor a place to start.

Invite them to meet Northern Light while you are still here to be part of the conversation.

They can ask questions. You can explain what matters. And when the time comes, they already know who to call.

What to organize
Will & key documents
Assets & liabilities
Bank & investment accounts
Insurance & bequests
Lawyer, accountant & key contacts
Companies, trusts & other interests

Good estate planning prepares the documents.
EstateReady prepares the people.

Phase 1 · Family continuity

When a family
becomes an estate.

Every estate begins the same way: with the loss of someone you love.

You are allowed to grieve
and to be deeply human.

In the first days, an Executor is rarely thinking like an Executor. They are still a grieving spouse, son, daughter, sibling, parent or friend.

You do not have to solve everything at once.

There will be financial and legal responsibilities, but not everything needs to happen at once. First, life still needs to function.

Keep life functioning
Pay the mortgage
Keep the lights on
Buy groceries
Plan the funeral
Support one another

With the current Will and an Estate Investment Management Agreement (Estate IMA) in place, Northern Light and the Executor can get to work while probate continues.

Phase 3 · Estate stewardship

Completing the stewardship.

By the time probate is granted, much of the financial work may already be underway. Probate allows the Executor to move toward beneficiary distributions and bring the estate toward completion.

While probate is underway
Investments remain managed
Cash can be raised
Taxes and estate obligations can be paid
Once probate is granted
Beneficiary distributions can begin
Remaining assets can be transferred
The estate can move toward completion

Northern Light continues working alongside the Executor, lawyer and accountant through final distributions and the remaining financial work.

The objective is not simply to distribute assets.

It is to complete a lifetime of stewardship with the same care that shaped it.
