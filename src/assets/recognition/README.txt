Award and press logos for the Recognition section on the home page.

Drop the files in here with exactly these names and they appear on the site at
the next build. No code change is needed. Until a file is here, that mark is
set as its name in type instead.

  wealth-professional.png     Wealth Professional
  cpa-alberta.png             CPA Alberta
  top-under-40.png            Top Under 40
  advisor-ca.png              Advisor.ca
  cpa-dividends.png           CPA Dividends
  investment-executive.png    Investment Executive
  globe-and-mail.png          The Globe and Mail

.png, .jpg, .svg and .webp all work — if you have a different extension, change
the name in src/data/recognition.js to match.

Use a transparent PNG or an SVG where you can. The marks are drawn in grey at
62% and come to full colour on hover, so a logo baked onto a white rectangle
will show as a pale block against the page.

Around 600px on the long edge is plenty; they are drawn at roughly 150x72.
